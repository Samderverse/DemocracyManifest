# Democracy Manifest

A tribute game to one of the great viral videos. A distinguished gentleman tries to finish his succulent Chinese meal while officers try to bundle him into the van.

- **EAT** (Space): take a bite. You can't block while you chew.
- **BLOCK** (A / D): parry when a hand glows `!`. Mash to shake free if grabbed.
- Clear 5 courses, picking an Eloquence card between each.

Soundbites live in `audio/bite01–19.mp3`, cut from the source clip at its natural pauses. They play in a shuffled order on grabs, parries and course wins, and they're all on the Soundboard screen.
